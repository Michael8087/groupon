GROUPON · PERSONALIZATION

Senior Product Manager, Personalization

Case study · Confidential · For candidate use only

The situation

Groupon's experience is mostly standardized: every customer sees the same homepage, search, deal pages and checkout. But customers come with different needs, and from different places: the app, the web, search, email.

Most of the time we don't know what they came for. We tried asking with an onboarding quiz, and few people finish it.

  • Millions of customers a month; hundreds of thousands of deals across local services, things to do, travel and goods.
  • Your team: 3 engineers, half a data scientist, half a designer.
  • Our company goals are growing margin, and getting more customers to buy again within 30 days.
Build the system that decides what each customer sees. Make it smarter every time they come back, and show us how you'd know it's making money.

Your task

  1. Your customers
    • Based on your research, pick the 3 segments you would focus on, and explain why.
    • For each segment: what do you need to know about these customers? What do you think we already have, what's missing, and how would you find out the missing part?
    • Which segment would you start with, and why?
  2. Build a prototype the main taskUse AI tools to build something that works, not just pictures of screens.
    • Pick one page on mobile to work on: the homepage, search, or a deal page.
    • Show what that page looks like for different customers, and explain why each part is there.
    • Show the same customer coming back 3 times in a month. What does the prototype learn about them each time, and how does the page change?
    • Show how it decides what each customer sees: what it knows about them, and the logic that turns that into the page.
    • Show what a customer we know nothing about would see.
  3. Draw the system
    • Draw one diagram of how it would work at Groupon: where the data comes from, how it flows, where decisions are made, and what tools or tech you'd use.
    • Walk one customer through it: they open the app. What happens, step by step, until they see the page?
    • What would you prioritize in the first 3 months? What would you deliberately not do yet, and why?
  4. Prove it worksHow would you measure success? What would you track, what must not get worse, and what result would make you stop? How would you test it before rolling it out to everyone?
  5. Challenge yourselfPre-mortem: it's 6 months later and this failed. What's the most likely reason?
    Contrarian: what does everyone assume about personalization that you think is wrong for Groupon?
Heads-up: in the interview, we'll ask you to change your system live, on screen, in about 15 minutes. Build it so you can.
